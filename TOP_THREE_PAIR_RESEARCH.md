# Manual review of the three highest-ranked pairs

Reviewed 2026-09-27 against the app's 2026-09-26 dataset. These are the default score-ranked, qualifying pairs after the default filter hides pairs whose two target dates have passed. All three involve DESC project 6888; they are three alternative pair leads, not three independent DESC jobs.

## Sources checked

- [DESC 2026–2030 public project list](https://www.scrtp.com/assets/pdfs/home/2026-2030-2million-and-above-project-descriptions.pdf), project 41 of 54, PDF p. 41: project 6888, proposed Deerfield switching station and series reactor on the Okatie–McIntosh 115 kV tie; planned 12/31/2028; $5,376,418 estimate; $50,000 allocated to 2027 and $5,326,418 to 2028.
- [Georgia PSC filing 225600](https://psc.ga.gov/search/facts-document/?documentId=225600), filed 2026-02-27. The public attachment is the 2025 GA ITS Ten-Year Plan (2026–2035). Its detail pages 60, 66, and 183 cover TEAMS 21275, 21257, and 20065. The attachment is titled Public Disclosure, but pages carry a contradictory CEII/confidential footer; this review uses only the public project fields already represented in the app and corroborated by the regional public planning report.
- [2026 SERTP Preliminary Expansion Plan Report (Non-CEII)](https://www.southeasternrtp.com/docs/general/2026/2026_SERTP_Preliminary_Expansion_Plan_Report_%28Non-CEII%29.pdf), dated 2026-06-12, pp. 50, 53, and 54. It corroborates the three Georgia scopes and 2028 in-service year. The report's section headings also say CEII despite its Non-CEII title, so retain the source distinction when presenting it.
- [2025 SERTP Regional Transmission Plan](https://www.southeasternrtp.com/docs/general/2025/2025%20Regional%20Transmission%20Plan%20and%20Input%20Assumptions.pdf), p. 111, independently identifies the Goshen–Georgia-Pacific (Rincon) section for the line rebuild.
- App locations derive from the sponsor workbook and OpenStreetMap substation features, as recorded in `data/projects.json`. None of the cited planning records provides a verified Deerfield site coordinate or construction route.

## Implications for the demo

The schedules are **planning targets**, not confirmed construction commitments. The Georgia filing supplies explicit start and need dates; DESC supplies an in-service date and annual budget, from which the app infers a window. The app's displayed 670-day overlap for pairs 1–2 and 517-day overlap for pair 3 are model outputs, not joint schedules published by the utilities.

Prioritize finding a public, defensible Deerfield location and correcting TEAMS 20065's mapped work section. Until then, present the first two as geographic screening leads and flag the third's position as uncertain. No source reviewed establishes a shared worksite or verified corridor; the corresponding score bonuses should remain zero.
