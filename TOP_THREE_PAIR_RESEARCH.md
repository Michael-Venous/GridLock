# Manual review of the leading pair candidates

Reviewed 2026-09-27 against the app's 2026-09-26 dataset. The current default ranking puts four pairs involving **the same DESC project, 6888**, first. These are alternative partner leads for one project, not four independent DESC jobs. Ranks were recalculated from `data/projects.json` with `src/match.js`; the manual source review below covers projects 6888, 21275, 21257, and 20065. TEAMS 20989 was added to the ranking after Santee Cooper and location updates and has not had the same independent source review.

## Sources checked

- [DESC 2026–2030 public project list](https://www.scrtp.com/assets/pdfs/home/2026-2030-2million-and-above-project-descriptions.pdf), project 41 of 54, PDF p. 41: project 6888, proposed Deerfield switching station and series reactor on the Okatie–McIntosh 115 kV tie; planned 12/31/2028; $5,376,418 estimate; $50,000 allocated to 2027 and $5,326,418 to 2028.
- [Georgia PSC filing 225600](https://psc.ga.gov/search/facts-document/?documentId=225600), filed 2026-02-27. The public attachment is the 2025 GA ITS Ten-Year Plan (2026–2035). Detail pages 60, 66, 183, and 187 cover TEAMS 21275, 21257, 20065, and 20989. The attachment is titled Public Disclosure, but pages carry a contradictory CEII/confidential footer; this review uses only the public project fields represented in the app. Obtain a non-CEII corroborating source for any claim that is central to the demo.
- [2026 SERTP Preliminary Expansion Plan Report (Non-CEII)](https://www.southeasternrtp.com/docs/general/2026/2026_SERTP_Preliminary_Expansion_Plan_Report_%28Non-CEII%29.pdf), dated 2026-06-12, pp. 50, 53, and 54. It corroborates the Georgia scopes for TEAMS 21275, 21257, and 20065 and their 2028 in-service year. The report's section headings also say CEII despite its Non-CEII title, so retain the source distinction when presenting it.
- [2025 SERTP Regional Transmission Plan](https://www.southeasternrtp.com/docs/general/2025/2025%20Regional%20Transmission%20Plan%20and%20Input%20Assumptions.pdf), p. 111, independently identifies the Goshen–Georgia-Pacific (Rincon) section for the line rebuild.
- App locations derive from the sponsor workbook and OpenStreetMap features, as recorded in `data/projects.json`. None of these planning records gives a verified Deerfield site or construction route.
